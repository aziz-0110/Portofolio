    Case Study · Edge AI & Vision

    Rail Inspection System

    Problem

    Manual track inspection is slow and subjective, and findings are hard to tie to an exact position. This system detects defects automatically and logs each finding with its GPS position.

    My role

    I led a team of 6 students, supervised by 3 lecturers, and set the technical direction of the project. I owned the vision pipeline (detection and segmentation models), its deployment on the Jetson Orin NX, and the live web dashboard.

    Architecture

    Dual Insta360 Multiprocess capture Detection + Segmentation (TensorRT / ONNX) GPS-odometry slot counting WebSocket dashboard + CSV export

    Results

    14,176
    tiles in the defect dataset
    3
    defect classes: corrugation, shelling, squat
    2
    cameras, one Jetson Orin NX
    83.37%
    IoU (segmentation)
    87.15%
    mAP (object detection)
    30 FPS
    real-time on Jetson Orin NX

    Challenges & lessons

    • Fastener overcounting. Per-frame accumulation double-counted objects. Tracking evolved from multi-object tracking to Hungarian matching to a GPS-odometry slot architecture where vision only classifies each slot's condition.
    • Jetson heap corruption. A CUDA initialization race across spawned processes was fixed with sequential model loading, warm-up runs and graceful SIGTERM shutdown.
    • Dataset leakage. A dataset audit found near-duplicate tiles across splits, so I moved to a cluster-based resplit and per-brightness evaluation.
